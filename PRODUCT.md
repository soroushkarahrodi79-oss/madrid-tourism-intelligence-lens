# Madrid Urban Evidence Lens

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Madrid residents and local stakeholders start with a place and a question. Planners, researchers and analysts can verify the same evidence against its source, unit, date and geometry.

## Product Purpose

A place-first map for finding what official sources document about urban development in Madrid, comparing places at an explicit spatial scale and understanding the limits of each reading. Tourism remains one evidence domain.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The public entry leads directly to the bilingual Leaflet observatory. Shared readings, fragment links and guided cases open the viewer directly. Scientific claims, provenance and interpretation limits follow [Product semantics](docs/PRODUCT_SEMANTICS.md), [Claims and limitations](docs/CLAIMS_AND_LIMITATIONS.md) and [Data provenance](docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md).

## Product Principles

- Make official records accessible without changing their meaning.
- Keep scope, sources and limitations available to people who need to verify a reading.
- Treat the public entry as an invitation to explore Madrid. Its imagery is editorial.
