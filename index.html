        Sources

        Sources: Ayuntamiento de Madrid — IDEAM (administrative geography: districts, barrios), Subdirección General de Estadística / Padrón Municipal (registered residents), and Censo de Locales (Hospitality & Commercial Context, Sep 2026 fingerprinted snapshot); Madrid Open Data (museums, tourist information, principal parks, permanent pedestrian counters); EMT Madrid (BiciMAD); CRTM (Metro and Cercanías stations); and Madrid Destino / esmadrid.com (hotels and other accommodation). The accommodation catalogue remains separate from the administrative Division-55 premises class. GitHub Pages uses the latest deployment-time POI snapshot first so markers render immediately; a small curated repository sample is the final packaged fallback when a deployment source is unavailable. HATI-Madrid locked pilot evidence (21 Aug 2023; UTCI is model output, not field observation). Destination context: Instituto Nacional de Estadística — hotel occupancy survey and direct internal-tourism municipality workbook (Madrid municipality only; domestic origin crossings published only above 30 tourists) — see data provenance and claims & limitations. Base maps: OpenStreetMap/CARTO or Esri World Imagery, depending on selection.
