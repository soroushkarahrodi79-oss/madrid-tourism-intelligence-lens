            What this means

            This place sits inside an official planning ámbito — the area in which Madrid's general plan records development state and available buildability.

            The plan publishes four separate development values for it, shown below exactly as published.

            It also publishes how much buildability the plan makes available there, by use class, in m² edificable.

            All of it comes from one dated official edition, whose reference date is shown with the figures.

            What it does not say: nothing here measures physical construction, the four values do not combine into one stage or percentage, no number of homes is published or derivable, and no figure is a share of the Lens circle.

            Four independent published administrative phase values and available planning buildability in m² edificable, from one dated official edition, for the whole ámbito. A published phase state is an administrative state, never proof of physical construction. No overall stage, progress, percentage or timeline exists. No dwelling count is published or derivable. No figure is apportioned into the Lens circle, the barrio or any sub-area.

              What this is — and is not

              Granted is not built. A granted licence means an administrative grant was recorded; it is not evidence that work started, construction occurred or completed, occupancy happened, or the permitted quantity was built.

              Granted licences only (RESOLUCIÓN = Conceder). There is no applications or refusals denominator, so no approval, rejection or refusal rate exists or is derivable.

              The three families are never summed into a single cross-family total.

              Licence records may concern places also in the Censo de Locales, licensed VUT or hospitality evidence; these universes are never summed as unique businesses or sites.

              What this means

              These are urban licences the Ayuntamiento recorded as granted, placed at their official address and counted inside the Lens by family and grant date. A granted licence is an administrative step — not proof that anything has been built.

              Address-point provenance

              Current official callejero: barrio and district are the callejero's own codes.

              Historical-only recovery: the official historical coordinate places the point and its barrio is derived from the canonical polygons — a different provenance, kept distinct.

              Some resolved rows carry an address-text disagreement with the current callejero. Exact NDP identity is authoritative; no coordinate is moved and nothing is re-geocoded from the text.
